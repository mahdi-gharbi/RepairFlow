## 1. Vision and proximity sensors
The ARM-6 sorter uses a stereo camera for item detection and two proximity sensors for safety stops.

## 2. Detection errors
Error E-SNS-07 means the stereo camera calibration drifted beyond tolerance. Items are missed or picked off-center. Clean the camera window, then run the calibration routine with the reference target.

## 3. Arm vibration
Strong vibration while lifting usually comes from a loose gripper mount or a worn joint bearing. Stop the arm and inspect the mount before the next shift.
