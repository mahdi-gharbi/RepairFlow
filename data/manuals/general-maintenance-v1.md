## 1. Before opening a ticket
Collect the equipment id, the time of the event, the error code shown in telemetry and what the operator observed. Missing one of these slows down diagnosis.

## 2. Wheels and drive (AMR)
Grinding or squeaking from a wheel usually means debris in the caster or a worn wheel bearing. Error E-MOT-12 indicates a drive motor overcurrent.

## 3. Safety
Never bypass a safety stop. A robot that stopped by itself must be inspected before being restarted in production.
